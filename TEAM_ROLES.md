# Team Roles & Responsibilities
## Ri-Sō 理創 Research Program 2026 — Medical Imaging Track

---

## Mentor

**Role:** Supervisor and strategic guide. The mentor does not do hands-on technical work.

**Responsibilities:**
- Set the research direction and validate the team's approach at each milestone
- Review and confirm decisions made by the Associate Researcher before they are finalized
- Attend bi-weekly sprint reviews and provide strategic feedback
- Identify and resolve blockers that the team cannot handle on their own
- Sign off on final submissions, Docker containers, and challenge deliverables
- Ensure clinical and scientific integrity is maintained throughout the project

**Does NOT:**
- Write code or run experiments directly
- Assign or manage day-to-day tasks (delegated to Associate Researcher)
- Handle logistics or administrative matters (delegated to Admin)

---

## Associate Researcher

**Role:** Technical lead and primary bridge between the team and the mentor.

**Responsibilities:**
- Work hands-on with the team across all sprint milestones
- Assign tasks to team members, track progress, and unblock day-to-day issues
- Run experiments, review code, and validate results before escalating to the mentor
- Prepare and present sprint updates to the mentor for confirmation and validation
- Ensure the team follows the sprint plan and meets milestone deadlines
- Draft the challenge submission report and paper methodology sections

**Relationship with Mentor:**
- The Associate Researcher surfaces findings, results, and decisions to the mentor
- The mentor confirms, redirects, or validates — the Associate Researcher executes
- Nothing is finalized without mentor confirmation on key decisions

---

## Admin (Board Member)

**Role:** Logistics coordinator and operational support.

**Responsibilities:**
- Handle all logistical matters: compute resource requests, access provisioning, scheduling
- Maintain the team's project board and track task completion status
- Coordinate team meetings, communicate deadlines, and send progress reports to program admins
- Manage platform submissions (Codabench, Grand-Challenge, Synapse) from a logistics standpoint
- Act as the point of contact between the team and the broader IEEE E-JUST EMBS SBC board
- Escalate operational blockers (access issues, resource shortages) to the appropriate authority

**Does NOT:**
- Make technical decisions or validate research output
- Supervise or assess team members' technical work

---

## Team Member (TM1 – TM8)

**Role:** Core researcher and implementer.

**Responsibilities:**
- Execute assigned tasks within their sprint milestone as directed by the Associate Researcher
- Implement, test, and document their component of the pipeline
- Submit completed work (with a file link) through the dashboard for review
- Attend team standups and flag blockers immediately — do not stay stuck for more than one day
- Iterate based on feedback from the Associate Researcher or Mentor after review
- Contribute to the final paper and challenge submission as directed

**Escalation path:**
- Blocker on a task → raise immediately to the Associate Researcher
- Associate Researcher cannot resolve → Associate Researcher escalates to Mentor
- Logistical issue (access, scheduling) → raise to Admin
